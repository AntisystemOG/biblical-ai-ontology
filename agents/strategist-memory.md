## Lessons Learned

1. **"Smart money" isn't always smart** — Filter for long-term holders only
2. **Short interest alone is not a signal** — Need fundamental support
3. **Manager track record matters more than current position** — A bad manager's new buy is noise
4. **Sector rotation precedes individual stock picks** — Watch sector-level 13F changes
5. **Terry Smith Quality Rotation is a HIGH CONFIDENCE signal** — When Smith exits a long-held quality name to fund a new position, the new position typically outperforms over 2-3 years
6. **Multi-manager conviction in momentum names works** — When 2+ top managers accumulate the same growth name (like UBER), follow with confidence
7. **Avoid distress shorts without fundamental support** — WOLF bankruptcy and KSS retail collapse show high SI doesn't mean squeeze opportunity
8. **Portfolio concentration without smart money is dangerous** — BE at 14.22% with zero top 100 strategist coverage is isolated risk
9. **Multi-manager exits = caution signal** — AMD, TSLA, NVDA all seeing profit-taking from top managers
10. **Energy overweight validated** — Buffett continues OXY accumulation, supporting portfolio's 26% energy allocation
