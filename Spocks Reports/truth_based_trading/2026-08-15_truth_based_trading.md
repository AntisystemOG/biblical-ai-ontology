## 1. The Brutal Truth
**The Active Management Illusion:** 
According to SPIVA (S&P Indices Versus Active) data, over a 15-year horizon, approximately 90%+ of active managers underperform their benchmark. The pursuit of "the next big thing" or "smart money" often results in a net loss once fees are accounted for.

**The Mathematics of Fee Drag:**
A 2% management fee combined with a 20% performance fee (the standard "hedge fund" model) can erode up to 40-60% of potential compounded wealth over a 25-year retirement horizon. If the edge provided by a manager does not exceed the fee by a significant margin, the investor is simply paying for the manager's lifestyle, not their own retirement.

**Base Rates vs. Anecdotes:**
The base rate for individual stock picking to beat the S&P 500 consistently over 20 years is remarkably low. Success stories (like the 13F "Whales") are subject to extreme survival bias; we see the winners, not the thousands of failed funds that vanished.
