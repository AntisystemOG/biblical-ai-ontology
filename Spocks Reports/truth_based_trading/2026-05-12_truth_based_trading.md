## Part 4: The Hard Rules

### Entry Rules
1. **Never buy on news** — priced in by the time you hear it
2. **Never buy because someone famous did** — 90-day lag
3. **Never buy without reading the 10-K** — know what you own
4. **Never buy at all-time highs** — patience is an edge

### Exit Rules
1. **Sell when thesis breaks** — not when price drops
2. **Sell when valuation exceeds reality** — MU at +38% weekly
3. **Sell when you need the money** — retirement, not panic
4. **Never sell winners to buy losers** — hold your flowers

### Holding Rules
1. **Minimum 5-year horizon** — or don't buy
2. **Rebalance annually** — not daily
3. **Ignore the scoreboard** — 10-year games have noise
4. **Add on weakness** — if thesis intact
