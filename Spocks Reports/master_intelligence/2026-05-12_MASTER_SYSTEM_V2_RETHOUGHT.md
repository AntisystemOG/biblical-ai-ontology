## Part 6: Behavioral Guardrails

### The "Don't Panic" Checklist
Before selling any position, answer:
1. **Has the thesis broken?** (Not just price down)
2. **Would I buy more at this price?** (If yes, hold)
3. **Am I selling because of fear?** (If yes, wait 48 hours)
4. **What would Buffett/Smith do?** (Follow their time horizon)

### The "Don't Get Greedy" Checklist
Before buying any position, answer:
1. **Is this FOMO?** (Did it already run 50%+?)
2. **Do I understand the business?** (10-K level understanding)
3. **What's my exit criteria?** (Know before buying)
4. **Am I chasing a narrative?** ("AI pivot" = red flag)

### The "Sleep Test"
If a position causes sleep loss:
- **Reduce size** (not eliminate)
- **Set stop loss** (mechanical exit)
- **Re-evaluate thesis** (maybe too speculative)
