- Center covers: the numbers both sides skip — Brent level, yield moves, September seasonality (historically worst month).

**Likely Reality:** A tit-for-tat cycle both sides think they can control. Markets are pricing recurring escalation, not full war: oil at $95 (not $200), gradual yield rise. Watch Hormuz — that's the tail risk.
