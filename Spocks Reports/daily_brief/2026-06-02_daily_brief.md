### Story 1: Perpetual Futures Approval Rocks Exchange Stocks

**What happened:** The CFTC cleared the way for U.S. trading of perpetual ("perp") futures on cryptocurrencies. On Tuesday, exchange operators sold off sharply: Cboe Global Markets -9%, CME Group -4%, Intercontinental Exchange (NYSE parent) -4%. Investors fear perps will siphon volume from traditional futures and introduce systemic risk.

| **Right (Fox/Newsmax/NYPost)** | Also largely silent on this story today. NY Post focused on Dow/politics; Newsmax on SEC climate rule repeal. | Missing: any deregulation-praise angle — unusual given this is a CFTC deregulatory move under Trump. |

**Likely reality:** The selloff is an overreaction to headline risk. Perps are already dominant offshore; bringing them onshore may actually *increase* CME/Cboe revenue long-term through new product engineering. Short-term panic, medium-term neutral-to-positive for incumbent exchanges.
