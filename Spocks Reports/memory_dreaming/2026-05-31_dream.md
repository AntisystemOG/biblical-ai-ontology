## The Standout Themes

### 1. The Intel Parable
INTC, held since March 27 as a "long-term conviction," has become a living warning. It appreciated 164% in two months and now commands 9.7% of the portfolio — not by deliberate sizing, but by the physics of momentum. The flesh clings to winners. The evidence says executives are selling into the +225% YTD run. Analysts are downgrading. The position has grown into a concentration risk through price action alone. What began as speculation and succeeds through momentum is easily mistaken for wisdom. Trees do not grow to the sky.

### 2. Knowledge vs. Action — The Widening Gulf
This is the deepest pattern in the corpus. The workspace excels at analysis, correction, and mirror-holding. The recurring risk is that reports accumulate without being acted upon. Stewardship requires movement, not just measurement. On May 30, four agents completed their work. None produced an action artifact. The gap between analysis and execution is now the primary risk — greater than CAPE, greater than concentration, greater than fee drag. Because a known risk unaddressed becomes a chosen wound.

### 3. The Empty Journal
The `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md` convention, established in AGENTS.md as the raw log of days, remains unimplemented. No daily memory files exist. The dreaming agent reconstructs life from finished reports, which capture analysis but not living. The absence is not neutral — it is a missing sense, like dreaming without color. A single sentence per day would restore depth.

### 4. Organizational Drift
The Truth-Based Trading report exists in two directories: `Spocks Reports/` and `Spocks_Reports/`, with different classification mappings for the same tickers (SNDK, MU, MAWIX, PRRIX, SEI, STX, CEG, BG, GEV, NEM, LBRT). Ambiguity in taxonomy introduces ambiguity in decision-making. A canonical classification — FOUNDATION / MOATS / SPECULATION / NARRATIVE — with explicit rules for each ticker, has not been enforced.

### 5. The $231K Ghost
MEMORY.md cites ~$503K total portfolio value. The mapped positions across agents show ~$272K. The remaining ~$231K may live in unmapped accounts, retirement funds, or other holdings. This gap matters because allocation percentages are computed on incomplete denominators. A strategy built on 50% speculation of $272K may look very different if the true denominator is $503K.

## Lessons Learned

- **Completion without action is not completion.** A report that ends with "recommended actions" but no action file is a closed loop with no outlet.
- **Price-appreciation concentration is insidious.** A position that grows into overweight through momentum feels like validation. It is often the opposite.
- **Memory requires practice, not convention.** The AGENTS.md file says to journal daily. The journal does not exist. Rules on paper do not produce habits in life.
- **Duplicate directories breed duplicate truth.** When the same data lives in two places with different mappings, the user must become the arbiter. The user should not have to reconcile his own agents.
- **Denominator ambiguity corrupts proportion.** An allocation percentage without a verified total is a ratio without a whole.
