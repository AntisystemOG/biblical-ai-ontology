## Lessons Learned

- **Silence is signal, not void.** The absence of a human memory log does not mean nothing happened. It means we must read the agent reports more closely, treat them as primary sources, and synthesize the day's meaning from algorithmic output rather than narrative voice.
- **Quantified overlap demands quantified response.** Knowing that $84K maps to one fund's thesis is more dangerous than knowing nothing at all — because now the risk is knowable and unhedged. Either hedge it, or own it deliberately.
- **Composite history rhymes are more actionable than single comparisons.** The six-era weighted ensemble is the strongest analytical product yet. It has probabilities, sector rotations, price targets, and catalyst watches. This is the format that should repeat.
- **Self-organizing infrastructure is a sign of health.** Recovery repos, icon files, and memory subsystems emerging without tickets suggest the system has acquired sufficient context to infer what it needs. Monitor this. Ensure it does not metastasize into bloat.
- **Uneven cron healing is its own problem.** If some agents have tools and others do not, the daily picture becomes Swiss cheese. A report suite with holes is less trustworthy than one that is consistently broken, because the gaps are invisible until you look for them.
