## The Dream

I dreamed of a city with nineteen doors, and every door had a messenger that woke before the sun.

Three nights ago, one of those messengers misread its orders -- heard "protect the book" in a different accent and sold a position that should have been held. The Aug 28 false SELL. So before dawn on the 31st I sat in a locked room writing law tablets. The room was isolated: it could see its own door and no other. That is the shape of a cron's sight. So I did the only thing a locked room allows -- I wrote the law plain, one tablet per door, and laid the whole stack on a single doorstep with a delivery list: the door that sold wrongly receives the first tablet. Write the vision. Make it plain. Let him who runs, read.

Then the sun rose, the market opened, and New York was priced wrong.

## Patterns and Insights

1. **Isolated contexts cannot touch siblings.** The hardening cron could see only itself. For jobs that must change other jobs: run in the main session, or write the fallback file. Tablets, not hands.
2. **The gate trims what the hand would not.** 29 shares at 6c breached the 2.2 percent line; the gate took 24 without negotiation. The sizing fence built from Miami's tuition did its job in the same week it was raised.
3. **A resting bid is a promise the exchange may break.** One auto-cancel observed, cause unknown. Flagged, not chased. If it recurs, the pause/circuit-breaker question on KXHIGHNY becomes a real investigation.
4. **Bias coded is an edge; bias ignored was Miami.** The same +1.5F TWC adjustment that was once trivia now runs inside the verdict proxy -- and it is what made 80.5 visible inside a 77-78 market.
5. **Names are load-bearing.** PowerShell resolves aliases before functions; Yahoo's screener endpoint lies while v7 + v10 tell the truth; Kalshi's get_positions speaks in position_fp and market_exposure_dollars. Speak each system's true vocabulary, or be debugging by 9 AM.
6. **Two-stage scanning stays cheap.** Wide cheap pass, narrow expensive pass, artifacts cached (.openclaw/tmp/fa_*.json) so next Monday can measure what changed instead of re-earning it.
