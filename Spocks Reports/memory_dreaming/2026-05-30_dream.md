## Lessons Learned

**For the workspace:**
- Daily memory files (`memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md`) have not been created. The infrastructure exists in `AGENTS.md` but the practice does not. The riverbed is dry.
- A single comprehensive report (truth-based trading) is worth more than scattered fragments, but continuity suffers without daily notes.
- The difference between Thad's total portfolio (~$503K in MEMORY.md) and the truth-based trading snapshot (~$271K) needs clarification—likely different account scopes, but ambiguity creates drift.

**For the soul:**
- The Spock persona is not a mask; it is a discipline of logic held in submission to Scripture. That tension is good. It is meant to be uncomfortable.
- The portfolio is a parable: half the wealth is placed on numbers that SPIVA says will lose. Thad knows this. The question is whether he will *move*.
