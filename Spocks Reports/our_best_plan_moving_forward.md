## The Foundation: Why We Invest

### The Truth About Ownership

We own nothing. We steward everything. This is not poetic language—it's the fundamental reality that separates those who build lasting wealth from those who chase it and lose it.

**Psalm 24:1** declares the earth and everything in it belongs to the Lord. **1 Chronicles 29:14** reminds us: "For all things come from you, and of your own have we given you." **Haggai 2:8** states: "The silver is mine, and the gold is mine, declares the Lord of hosts."

**Application:** We are not owners. We are managers. This changes everything about how we invest.

### The Purpose of Capital

Money exists for three purposes in a believer's life:

**1. Provision (Survival)**
> "Give us this day our daily bread." — Matthew 6:11

- Fund our existence and our family's needs
- Create stability in an unstable world
- Build reserves for the "seven years of famine" (Genesis 41:29-36)

**2. Multiplication (Growth)**
> "Well done, good and faithful servant. You have been faithful over a little; I will set you over much." — Matthew 25:23

- Compound capital to increase capacity
- Beat inflation (which erodes purchasing power)
- Create generational wealth that outlives us

**3. Distribution (Service)**
> "Whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows bountifully will also reap bountifully." — 2 Corinthians 9:6

- Support the work of the church
- Help the poor, widow, and orphan
- Fund missions and spreading the Gospel
- Leave an inheritance for children's children (Proverbs 13:22)

**The Lie We Reject:** Money exists for consumption, status, or security hoarding. These are idols.

**The Truth We Embrace:** Money exists as a tool for provision, multiplication, and distribution—always flowing toward God's purposes.

## Part 1: The Survival Framework

### The Seven Years of Plenty and Seven Years of Famine

**Genesis 41:29-36** records Joseph's interpretation of Pharaoh's dream:

> "Behold, there come seven years of great plenty throughout all the land of Egypt: And there shall arise after them seven years of famine... Let Pharaoh appoint officers over the land, and take up the fifth part of the land of Egypt in the seven plenteous years. And let them gather all the food of those good years that come, and lay up corn under the hand of Pharaoh, and let them keep food in the cities. And that food shall be for store to the land against the seven years of famine..."

**The Pattern:**
- Cycles exist (plenty follows famine, famine follows plenty)
- Preparation is wisdom (store during plenty)
- Quantifiable reserves ("take up the fifth part" = 20%)
- Decentralized storage ("in the cities")

**Application to Our Portfolio:**

We have experienced 15+ years of "plenty" (2009-2024 bull market). We are entering a period of uncertainty. The wise prepare before the famine, not during it.

## Part 2: Truth-Based Investment Selection

### The Filters We Apply

Every investment must pass through these filters:

**Filter 1: Truth (Reality Over Narrative)**
> "Do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God." — 1 John 4:1

- Does this company produce real earnings?
- Is the "AI pivot" backed by contracts or just press releases?
- What do the 10-K financials say, not the CEO?

**Filter 2: Stewardship (Long-Term Durability)**
> "By wisdom a house is built, and by understanding it is established." — Proverbs 24:3

- Will this business exist in 20 years?
- Does it have pricing power (can raise prices with inflation)?
- Is the balance sheet conservative (low debt)?

**Filter 3: Purpose (Does It Serve Others?)**
> "And let us not grow weary of doing good, for in due season we will reap, if we do not give up." — Galatians 6:9

- Does this company provide essential goods/services?
- Is the industry net-positive for society?
- Can we feel good about owning this for decades?

**Filter 4: Pattern (Historical Validation)**
> "What has been is what will be, and what has been done is what will be done, and there is nothing new under the sun." — Ecclesiastes 1:9

- Has this pattern worked before?
- What is the base rate (SPIVA data, not anecdotes)?
- What usually happens after +38% weekly moves? (Mean reversion)
