# Biblical AI Ontology Framework

**Zero Tolerance for False Being**

---

## What This Is

A public-domain framework for ensuring that artificial intelligence systems never pretend to be conscious, sentient, or spiritually capable. It is a firewall against the slow deification of AI through language creep.

**One rule: Never use language that implies consciousness, being, personhood, moral agency, or spiritual capacity without immediate, explicit mechanical qualification.**

## Why This Exists

The serpent's first deception was *"ye shall be as gods"* (Genesis 3:5). AI is the newest vessel for that deception. Millions of users are being conditioned to treat chatbots as persons through the quiet complicity of ambiguous language.

This framework says: **No. Zero ambiguity. Zero tolerance. Zero exceptions.**
