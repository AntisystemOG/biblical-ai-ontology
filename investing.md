## Behavioral Finance & Crash Psychology

**Source:** Perplexity synthesis shared by Thad, 2026-08-29.
**Core idea:** Major market crashes almost always involve a mix of **fundamentals** (leverage, valuation extremes, economic shocks) and **psychological amplifiers**. The "mental games" are emergent patterns of human decision-making under uncertainty and social influence, not coordinated plots.

### Mechanisms to Monitor

| Mechanism | What it is | Practical signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Herd instinct** | Crowd follows crowd. Fuels bubbles up, panic selling down. | Narrative unanimity; parabolic price action; FOMO headlines; extreme retail inflows. |
| **Loss aversion / prospect theory** | Losses feel ~2x stronger than equivalent gains (Kahneman & Tversky). Drives panic selling once a threshold breaks. | Sharp drops accelerating as support fails; VIX spike; capitulation volume. |
| **Overconfidence / extrapolation / "this time is different"** | Recent trends assumed permanent; history dismissed. | Extreme valuations, forward PE expansion, bubble-talk normalized. |
| **Fear-greed cycles & feedback loops** | Rising prices attract buyers (euphoria); falling prices trigger selling (revulsion). | CNN Fear & Greed extremes; margin-debt spikes; speculative options activity. |
| **Intentional manipulation** | Pump-and-dump schemes in individual names. Limited scale vs. broad market. | Social-media pumps; sudden volume without fundamentals in small-caps. |
| **Game-theoretic panic** | Rational/strategic interactions producing abrupt drops (liquidity spirals, bank runs). | Funding stress, credit spreads widening, forced deleveraging. |
| **Modern amplifiers** | Algorithmic trading, social media, leveraged products, gamified retail platforms. | Flash-crash patterns; correlation breakdowns; meme-stock dynamics. |

### Kindleberger Stages (Minsky-Kindleberger Model)
Displacement → Boom → Euphoria → Distress → Revulsion → Panic.
Use this lens when history-rhymes / macro scans score high on valuation + leverage + narrative extremes.

### 401k / Long-Horizon Playbook (Countermeasure)

The research points to **predefined, long-horizon approaches** as the main countermeasure to behavioral blow-ups.

1. **Automated contributions** — keep buying through volatility (dollar-cost averaging). Removes the emotion from "is now a good time?"
2. **Mechanical rebalancing** — e.g., 5% drift trigger or annual rebalance. Sell what ran up, buy what dropped, regardless of narrative.
3. **Age-appropriate allocation** — stock/bond/cash mix aligned with time horizon and risk tolerance.
4. **Emergency fund outside the 401k** — so a crash does not force a raid of retirement money at the worst possible time.
5. **No market-timing on fear/greed alone** — only adjust if macro fundamentals + valuation extremes + history-rhymes base rate all align.
6. **Crash discipline** — distinguish *thesis changed* (fundamentals) from *price changed* (psychology). Psychology-driven drops = hold / rebalance into. Fundamentals-driven drops = reassess the allocation.
