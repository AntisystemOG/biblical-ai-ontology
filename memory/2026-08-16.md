## Denver Back-Out Analysis (06:28 CDT)

**What happened:** Backed out of Denver NO >87°F. It settled at 80°F actual high — we would have WON if we held.

**Why the forecast missed:**
- Forecast said 84°F. Actual high was 80°F.
- Thunderstorm at 2:23 PM killed heating. Temp dropped from 80 to 73 to 66.
- East wind all morning = upslope cooling. Signal missed.
- Market priced >87°F at 78-91% YES. Market was completely wrong.

**Lessons:**
1. Thunderstorms cap Denver highs 5-10 degrees below forecast. Check storm probability, not just forecast high.
2. East/upslope wind in Denver = cooler. Check wind direction.
3. Market overestimates Denver heat — weights historical norms over actual conditions.
4. Called 55% win prob when it was actually ~70%. Underconfidence is also a mistake.
5. Backing out made $4.42 PnL but holding would have made more. The call to back out was wrong.

**Rule:** When thunderstorm probability >40% in Denver, subtract 5 degrees from forecast high before evaluating edge.

## NYC Loss Analysis (21:34 CDT)

**What happened:** NYC NO on 78-79°F LOST. Actual high was 79°F — landed exactly in the band.

**Why:**
- Forecast said 80-81°F. Actual peaked at 79°F at 11:51 AM.
- Clouds rolled in at 12:51 PM (overcast), temps never climbed above 79.
- NWS observations show overcast from morning onward, light rain started at 3 PM.
- The cloud deck capped temps at 79 instead of reaching the forecast 81.
- Band bets (78-79) are dangerous because a 2-degree window is narrow. If forecast is 81 and actual lands at 79, you lose by 2 degrees.

**Lessons:**
1. Band bets are riskier than threshold bets. A 2-degree band can be hit by a small forecast miss. Avoid band bets when forecast is within 3 degrees of the band.
2. Cloud cover caps temps. NYC was overcast all morning — I should have checked cloud cover, not just forecast high.
3. When forecast high (81) is within 3 degrees of the band edge (79), the risk is too high. Need >4 degrees of cushion for band bets.
4. East/onshore wind in NYC = cooler (was variable/calm, but overcast dominated).

**New rule:** For weather band bets, require >4 degrees between adjusted forecast and band edges. If <4 degrees, PASS. Band bets are inherently riskier than threshold bets.

**Updated weather model:** Added band bet safety rule to weather_model.py logic.

## Weather Reference Guide (21:42 CDT)
Built `WEATHER_REFERENCE_GUIDE.md` with city-specific factors for Denver, NYC, Miami, Chicago, Dallas, Houston, Phoenix, LA.

Key factors per city:
- **Denver**: Elevation + afternoon storms cap temps 5-10°F. E wind = cooler. Storm prob >40% = subtract 5°F.
- **NYC**: Urban heat island + cloud cover caps temps. Overcast morning = subtract 2-3°F. Sea breeze from E/SE = cooler.
- **Miami**: Stable, rarely 95+. E/SE sea breeze caps temps. W/offshore = warmer. 95+ requires offshore + clear.
- **Chicago**: Lake Michigan breeze (E/NE) drops temps 5-10°F. W/SW = warmer.
- **Dallas**: Cold fronts from north. Inland = volatile.
- **Houston**: Gulf breeze caps temps, similar to Miami.
- **Phoenix**: Desert heat, monsoon storms can drop temps.
- **LA**: Marine layer caps temps. Santa Ana (E/NE) = extreme heat.

Mandatory pre-bet checklist: NWS observations, storm probability, cloud cover, wind direction, city adjustments, cushion check.

Band bets need >4°F cushion from both edges. Threshold bets need >3°F.

Data: NWS observations = FACT. Generic forecast sites = SPECULATION.

Track record: 1W 2L 1 back-out. Weather is hard — stick to claims unless edge is clear.
